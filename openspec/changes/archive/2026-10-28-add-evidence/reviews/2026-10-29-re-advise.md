## Independent take

Written before opening the design, tasks, delta, or prior review; based only on Why, the living quote/ingest specs, and the cited schema/API code.

1. Pin additive, item-addressable research and notes so a second contribution preserves the first.
2. Pin a durable document reference and enough provenance to distinguish a source from our interpretation of it.
3. Pin stable identities and explicit duplicate/retry semantics for contributions and their item associations.
4. Pin which fields can change, which constitute history, and how corrections or removals affect that history.
5. Pin ownership and deletion behavior for evidence shared across items, including the existing item-delete path.
6. Pin atomic writes and failure behavior so rejected associations or item deletion cannot leave partial changes.
7. Refuse to repurpose quote URL, method, price, or checked-at history as mutable research metadata.
8. Refuse implicit rewrites of legacy item source/notes or changes to ingest identity, rollback, and quote exclusions.
9. Pin the REST/OpenAPI request, response, validation, and error contract before any UI or agent integration.
10. Accept the tradeoff of explicit evidence creation and linking over automatic extraction if it keeps provenance clear and the first increment bounded.

## Comparison and steelman

The contract answers the independent concerns with shared sources, deduplicated item links, and separate append-only facts and thoughts. Sources identify documents; enrichment values/bodies and thought bodies preserve what was recorded about them. Source and link retries reuse identity, while another enrichment or thought POST deliberately appends another observation. Corrections can therefore be later contributions; a revision graph, idempotency keys, and document snapshots are unnecessary for this increment.

Against my initial emphasis on preserving history, deleting history with its owning item is an explicit and coherent lifetime policy. Individual records remain immutable, and the amended transaction boundary protects history when deletion fails. Retaining unused source rows is a reasonable simplification. There is no need to require garbage collection or a particular cascade strategy.

Existing item source/notes remain intact during backfill and evidence writes. Explicit item PATCH remains the application step, ingest integration remains a separate change, and quotes retain their own provenance and ranking. Hono/Zod/OpenAPI construction and scenario coverage remain open implementation work, as they should at contract acceptance.

## R1–R3 resolution

1. **R1 — resolved.** Design “Backfill, boot, and reseed” and delta “Backfill existing source strings” now distinguish upgrade, fresh seed, and forced reseed. All three have scenarios, and the task to cover delta scenarios encompasses them. Inspection of `src/lib/server/db/index.ts` confirms migration precedes seed; `src/lib/server/db/seed.ts` confirms the separate forced-reset deletion path. The amended requirements now cover both code paths, require cleanup of every new item-dependent table, and require equivalent catalog links afterward. Trimmed identity, blank legacy values, and otherwise invalid legacy locators have explicit handling without rewriting legacy columns.
2. **R2 — resolved.** Design “Delete” and delta “Item delete removes its evidence and keeps shared sources” require one transaction for quotes, all evidence joins/rows, and the item, preserving the BOM-in-use guard. Rollback is explicit, with a failure-after-quote-removal scenario. Separate scenarios preserve sources reached through bare links, enrichments, and thoughts on other items. This closes the partial-history-loss gap in the currently nontransactional handler.
3. **R3 — resolved.** Design “Time” and both append-only delta requirements pin stable ids, server-assigned `createdAt`, OpenAPI exposure, and ordering by timestamp then id. The two-facts scenario verifies distinguishable, ordered records. The ordering is deterministic, including ties; it need not claim chronological insertion order within a timestamp tie or choose an authoritative fact.

The accompanying source-reference detail is also addressed: missing source ids on links, enrichments, and thought citations return 404 with no writes. New locator validation is separated from permissive legacy backfill. No remaining architecture blocker was found.
