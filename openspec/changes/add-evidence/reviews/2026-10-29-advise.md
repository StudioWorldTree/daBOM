## Independent take, written before comparison

These ten lines were persisted in `2026-10-29-independent-take.md` before opening the design, tasks, or proposed evidence spec. Inputs were proposal Why, the living quote and ingest specs, and the cited schema/deletion code.

1. Pin evidence to stable SKU identity while allowing several sources and several authored observations for one item.
2. Pin the distinction between an external document, a captured observation of it, and our interpretation; a URL alone cannot preserve changing content.
3. Pin history semantics explicitly: adding a later source or thought must preserve earlier material, and corrections need an intelligible policy.
4. Pin enough provenance to return to the source and distinguish document time, capture time, and note creation time where those exist.
5. Refuse to turn evidence into a second price history: quotes retain their URL, method, checked-at date, and append-only monetary fields.
6. Pin Hono/Zod/OpenAPI creation and retrieval contracts, including missing-item errors and validation of source references.
7. Pin ownership and deletion before adding foreign keys: item deletion currently removes quotes first, so evidence must not cause partial history loss on a failed delete.
8. Pin compatibility for existing source/notes values and ingest matching; new evidence must not silently overwrite seeded item metadata or write quotes.
9. Pin retry and duplicate semantics so callers know whether repeated submissions represent another observation or the same attachment.
10. Accept the tradeoff of a small evidence model over a document-management system if it preserves readable history and source traceability without requiring fetching or parsing on the server.

## Comparison and steelman

The author's separation is sound: reusable source identity, bare item links, factual observations, and authored thoughts have different jobs. The joins support both read directions without copying documents onto every SKU. Explicitly leaving item columns alone preserves ingest's existing match behavior; retaining quote provenance avoids a competing price model. Source/link deduplication and POST-as-append for observations answer the retry concern sufficiently for this node. A dedicated correction workflow or idempotency-key system is not required.

Against my initial take, document snapshots and capture timestamps would overreach the stated scope. A locator identifies a document; the enrichment value/body and thought body preserve what was recorded here. Accept that tradeoff without requiring fetches, hashes, document versioning, or an apply route. Likewise, deleting history with its owning item is an explicit product choice, not a violation of append-only individual records. The gaps below concern making those choices reliable.

## Required amendments

### R1 — Cover fresh seed and forced reseed, not only an upgrade migration

`design.md:25` specifies one migration, and delta lines 65–73 test only already-seeded data. But `src/lib/server/db/index.ts:31–35` migrates **before** seeding. On a fresh database the migration sees no items; subsequent catalog inserts get no evidence links. The same catalog would therefore expose different evidence depending on whether the database was upgraded or newly created.

There is also a second deletion path: `src/lib/server/db/seed.ts:13–18` deletes items during `DABOM_RESEED=1`. New item foreign keys can block that path unless their deletion policy or reset cleanup covers it. If cleanup cascades, the one-time migration still will not recreate links after reseeding.

Amend the design, delta, and tasks to cover existing-database upgrade, fresh boot, and forced reseed. Require consistent catalog source links, preservation of legacy columns during ordinary backfill, and a reset that handles all new joins without foreign-key failures. Verify all three lifecycle paths; do not require a particular helper or cascade implementation.

### R2 — Make deletion of an item and its history atomic

`design.md:27` says deletion works the same way as quotes; delta lines 75–83 specify the successful result only. The cited handler (`src/lib/server/api/app.ts:225–237`) currently checks the BOM, deletes quotes, and deletes the item in separate statements. Extending that sequence can commit quote/evidence removal before a later foreign-key failure, including a remaining `thought_sources` row or a newly added BOM reference. The failed request then leaves an existing item with missing history.

Require one atomic deletion boundary for the item, quotes, item-source links, enrichments, thoughts, and thought-source joins, with the existing BOM guard preserved. A failed deletion must leave them unchanged. Sources referenced solely through another item's enrichment or thought must survive too, not just sources with another bare link. Add a rollback scenario with failure after cleanup begins and preservation scenarios for all three source-reference paths. Retaining all source rows is an acceptable simple policy; orphan garbage collection is not required.

### R3 — Give append-only history a time and ordering contract

`design.md:11–13` and delta lines 23–57 define immutable rows but no creation time or read order. Two conflicting facts survive, yet the API need not expose which was recorded later. Quotes already separate insertion time from observation time (`src/lib/server/db/schema.ts:106–109`); the new history needs a minimal equivalent for its own records.

Specify stable record identifiers and server-assigned creation timestamps for enrichments and thoughts, expose them in the OpenAPI DTOs, and define a deterministic order for each returned history list, including ties. Add a scenario demonstrating two successive observations can be distinguished and ordered. This does not require document dates, a merged timeline, ranking, or automatic application of the latest fact.
